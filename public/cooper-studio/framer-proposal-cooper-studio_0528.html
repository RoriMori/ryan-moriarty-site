Adding a new project — what it looks like for you
01
Log in 30 seconds
Go to framer.com, sign in. Your site is there. No hosting dashboard, no plugins to update, nothing to maintain.
02
Open the content editor easy
Click "Content" in the sidebar. You see a clean list of all your projects. Each row is one project, same as a spreadsheet.
03
Add the project easy
Click "New item." Fill in the fields: project name, year, category, short description. Drag photos in. The layout is handled automatically — no design decisions on your end.
04
Preview before publishing easy
Hit "Preview" to see exactly how the new project looks on the live site. If something's off, edit the content. The design stays untouched.
05
Publish one click
One button. The site updates instantly. No code, no calls, nothing to break.
What you never touch
Typography, spacing, colors, hover effects, mobile layout, navigation, page structure, International Orange accents — all locked in the design layer. Your updates are content only: text, images, project details. The site cannot break from your end.

My process
01
Contract + kickoff
We sign the project agreement, confirm scope and timeline, and you create your Framer account. I'm added as a collaborator. From here, everything lives in your account.
02
Wireframes — in batches, with approval at each stage
We work through page layouts in three passes: homepage and navigation first, then the project template (the most important page — it repeats across all your work), then the remaining static pages (About, Process, Services, Contact). You approve each batch before the next begins. Nothing moves forward without your sign-off.
03
Content delivery — your call on timing
You have two options here. Option A: deliver the full content template and all project photos before build begins — site launches complete. Option B: deliver content for a defined starter set of projects (we agree on the number at contract time) and the static pages — site launches with that, you add the rest yourself after handoff. Either way, whatever you commit to needs to be fully delivered before build starts. Partial content mid-build creates rework.
04
Build + content population
I build the site in Framer against the approved wires and populate it with your content and photos. The design system, CMS structure, and all page layouts are locked in. You won't need to touch any of this layer after launch.
05
Your review — staging before anything goes live
You review the full site in Framer's staging environment before a single change hits the live URL. One round of revisions is included. Feedback goes in, changes are made, you approve.
06
DNS switch + launch
We do a 20-minute screen share. You log into Squarespace, I tell you exactly what to enter. cooper-studio.com points to Framer. Propagation takes 1-24 hours. Site goes live.
07
CMS walkthrough + handoff
A 30-minute session where I walk you through exactly how to add a new project, update content, and publish changes. By the end you can do it confidently on your own. This is the clean close — after this, the site is yours.
